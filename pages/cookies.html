1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember your preferences and understand how visitors use them. We also use your browser's local storage for the same purpose — it works like a cookie but never leaves your device.

2. Storage We Use

NamePurposeDuration
Theme preferenceRemembers your light/dark mode choice (browser storage only)Until cleared
Text-direction preferenceRemembers your LTR/RTL choice (browser storage only)Until cleared

That's it — we don't set advertising or tracking cookies ourselves.

3. Third-Party Content

Some pages load images and icons from third-party services (such as our image hosts and social platforms). Those services may set their own cookies or collect standard technical data under their own policies:

  • Image hosting for our dessert photography.
  • Social media links (Instagram, Facebook, X, Pinterest) — only active if you click through.

4. Managing Cookies

  • Clear site data anytime in your browser settings to reset theme and direction preferences.
  • Block third-party cookies in your browser — our site keeps working, though some images may load slower.
  • Use private/incognito mode to browse without storing anything afterwards.
