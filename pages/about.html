The Beginning

From a Home Kitchen
to a Sweet Empire

Whisk & Bite began in 2018 in founder Amelia Chen's tiny apartment kitchen. Armed with a piping bag and an obsession with Belgian chocolate, she started hand-crafting cake pops for friends' events.

Word spread quickly. By 2020, Amelia had her first studio space. By 2022, Whisk & Bite was the go-to dessert atelier for luxury weddings and corporate events across the city.

Today, our team of eight passionate artisans crafts thousands of bites each month — but the philosophy remains the same: every single bite deserves to be extraordinary.
