Before You Reach Out

Frequently Asked
Questions

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent orders, please email rush@whiskandbite.com or call directly.
Studio visits are by appointment only. We love meeting clients in person to discuss custom designs and can even arrange a tasting session. Please email or call to schedule.
Currently we deliver nationally via temperature-controlled overnight courier. International shipping is available for truffles and shelf-stable items only. Contact us for details.
Please always disclose allergies in your order form. While we offer gluten-free and vegan options, our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, eggs, and gluten. We cannot guarantee cross-contamination freedom for severe allergies.
Your satisfaction is our absolute priority. If you're unhappy for any reason — please contact us within 24 hours of receiving your order. We will either remake the order or issue a full refund.
