1. Introduction

Whisk & Bite ("we", "us", "our") creates handcrafted dessert bites for celebrations big and small. This policy explains what information we collect when you browse our website, place an enquiry, subscribe to our newsletter or order from us — and how we use and protect it.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details — your name, email address and phone number when you submit an enquiry, comment or newsletter form.
  • Order details — occasion, product choices, quantities, event dates and design notes you share for quotes and custom orders.
  • Preferences — theme (light/dark) and text-direction choices, stored only in your own browser.
  • Technical data — basic, anonymous information such as browser type and pages visited, used to improve the site.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare quotes and design mockups.
  • To fulfil, deliver and support your orders.
  • To send newsletters and sweet updates — only if you subscribe (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To improve our menu, website and customer experience.

4. Sharing Your Information

We never sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary: with delivery partners to fulfil your order, or when required by law. Our website also loads images and fonts from third-party services, which may receive standard technical data such as your IP address.

5. Data Retention

Enquiry and order records are kept for up to 24 months for customer service and accounting purposes, then deleted or anonymised. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Your Rights

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@whiskandbite.com — we respond within 5 business days.

7. Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; parents or guardians place all orders on their behalf.

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@whiskandbite.com or +1 (555) 123-4567, or visit our studio at 14 Confection Lane, Pastry District.
