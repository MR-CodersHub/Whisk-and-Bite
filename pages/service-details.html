Behind the Scene

How Your Cake Pops
Are Made

Every step is crafted with obsessive care. Here's what happens between your order and delivery.

1

Design Brief & Approval

We discuss your vision, event details and design preferences. A digital mockup is created for your approval before any baking begins.

2

Baking & Blending

Fresh cake is baked in your chosen flavour, cooled, then carefully blended with our house-made cream cheese frosting to the perfect consistency.

3

Shaping & Chilling

Each pop is hand-rolled into a perfect sphere, placed on premium food-safe sticks, and chilled overnight for the ideal firmness.

4

Dipping & Coating

Each pop is dipped in tempered Belgian chocolate or coloured candy coating, allowed to set, and receives a second dip for a flawless, crack-free shell.

5

Hand-Painting & Decoration

Our edible painting artists hand-paint each pop with food-safe colours, apply gold leaf, attach handcrafted sugar flowers, and add final sprinkle or glitter details.

6

Quality Check & Packaging

Every pop is inspected by our quality team, individually wrapped, and placed in our signature pink & gold gift boxes with ribbon, wax seal, and personalised card.
